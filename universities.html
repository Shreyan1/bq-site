For universities / A mentored venture cohort / Pilot first

A mentored venture cohort for your students.

For universities, entrepreneurship centres, incubation cells and faculty. BQ runs a cycle of about 12 weeks with your students: an industry partner’s challenge, a campus round run by a student chapter, a shortlist, a mentored cohort and a pitch finale with early-stage investors.

Start a conversation How a cycle works

Every programme starts with a pilot: one university, one industry partner and one cycle.

What students get

Real problems, with people who have built before.

Tech and business students work as one team, from a challenge brief to a room of investors.

  • A real challenge

    An industry partner’s brief, with a named owner who reviews the strongest solutions.

  • Mentors who stay

    Builder mentors work with a team every week. Senior mentors join for sessions in their own domain.

  • Demand, cost and the case

    Mentors test whether the problem is real, whether anyone will pay and whether an investor would back it.

  • Mixed teams

    Mixed teams of tech and business builders, two to four people each.

  • Selection

    BQ runs selection. The teams with the strongest problem and early evidence go through to the mentored cohort.

  • A pitch finale

    The strongest teams pitch to angels and early-stage investors matched to their stage.

The cycle

About twelve weeks, from a brief to a pitch.

Two cycles a year, each ending in a finale. The plan below is indicative for one campus; dates and team numbers are agreed with each university.

Mentors

Mentors at every stage.

Every team is screened first, and every ask of a mentor is specific and time-bound.

  1. Campus round and shortlistThe three founders run early sessions with every team, then make the selection.
  2. Builder mentorsYoung professionals who give one team weekly, hands-on support through the mentored cohort.
  3. Senior mentorsFounders, leaders and seasoned builders who give two or three sessions in their own domain.
  4. Pitch finaleSenior mentors and investors judge the pitches and give feedback.

What it takes

A cycle at your university.

What your university gets

  • A mentored venture cohort for your students
  • Student demos and an outcomes report
  • Evidence for your innovation and entrepreneurship reporting
  • A student-led BQ chapter

What we need from you

  • A campus lead from your faculty or incubation cell
  • A student chapter to run the campus round
  • A venue for the campus round
  • A signed agreement before launch

Three programme sizes, for small, medium and large campuses, with flexible payment terms. Pricing on request.

How it starts

Start with a pilot, then decide.

The first pilot is one university, one industry partner and one cycle.

  1. A scoping callWe learn about your students, your campus and what you want the cycle to achieve.
  2. Agree the planThe challenge brief, the campus and industry leads, the dates and the terms.
  3. Sign and launchThe agreement is signed and the challenge brief goes live.

Ground rules

Rules every team can trust.

  1. BQ runs selectionSelection is made by BQ, on the strength of each team’s problem and evidence.
  2. Teams own their workTeams own their ideas and their code.
  3. Introductions are opt-inEvery hiring or investment introduction is opt-in for the builder.
  4. No conflicts on the panelNo one judges a team they advise or plan to invest in.
  5. One view for every investorEvery investor sees the same teams at the same time.
