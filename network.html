Mentor the teams worth backing.

Two kinds of mentor

What you give, and what you get.

  • Young professionals

    Builder mentors

    Weekly, hands-on support for one team through the mentored cohort. In return: hands-on leadership experience, visibility through BQ’s channels and access to senior mentors and founders.

  • Founders, leaders and seasoned builders

    Senior mentors

    Two or three sessions in your own domain, and judging at the finale. In return: a first look at builders with opt-in hiring, the option to invest or advise as teams incorporate, a peer network of founders and leaders, a route to explore your own problems and an early view of what new builders make.

How mentors join

From a networking event to a role in a cycle.

Curated networking events build the mentor network, and each role in a cycle grows out of one.

  1. A curated eventFounders, leaders and builders meet the BQ team and each other.
  2. An opt-inWould you mentor, judge or bring a problem? You choose.
  3. One first askOne specific ask, with a date.
  4. A role in a cycleBuilder mentor, senior mentor or judge at the finale.

Small mentor roundtables between cycles keep people involved and bring in their peers.
