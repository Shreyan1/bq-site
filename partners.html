Ways to partner

Four ways to work with BQ.

  • Back an evening

    Host or sponsor an evening in Delhi NCR, and meet a room of researchers, engineers and investors. How you are recognised is agreed with you up front.

  • Take the evenings to a new city

    Help us start in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad or Pune once the format works in Delhi NCR.

  • Send a speaker or an engineer

    Your researchers lead an evening and your engineers run a Build Lab for a chapter. Your team meets people it may want to hire or build with.

  • Bring a live problem

    Share a real problem from your work with an evening or a chapter pod. We frame it with you, so you decide what is shared.

We listen before we pitch.

Every partnership starts with a conversation about what you need, and then we propose something specific. We don’t sell tiers or packages.
