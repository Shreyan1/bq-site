Ways to partner

Four ways to work with BQ.

  • Run a hackathon or flagship event

    We design it with you around a problem you care about and bring the strongest builders from our university chapters. How you are recognised is agreed up front.

  • Bring a live problem

    Share a real problem with a chapter pod and get usable work back. We frame it with you, so you decide what is shared.

  • Lend your engineers

    Your engineers mentor a pod or run a Build Lab, and meet people they may want to hire or build with.

  • Back a chapter or a city

    Sponsor a chapter at a university, or help take BQ to a new city or country.

We listen before we pitch.

Every partnership starts with a conversation about what you need, and then we propose something specific. We don’t sell tiers or packages.
