Builder’s Quorum isn’t another case competition or speaker series. It is the layer between exposure and venture creation, where you stay inside a sector long enough to build something real.

What you get

What a BQ sprint gives you.

Everything below happens inside one sprint, with the same pod and the same mentors from start to finish.

  • A live problem

    A real problem from a founder or company, framed with the people who own it.

  • Engineers and founders as mentors

    Direct mentorship from engineers, founders and operators at technology companies and startups.

  • Hands-on workshops

    Build Labs run by practitioners, where you leave with something that works.

  • A pod with the skills you lack

    Management, engineering and design students working as one team.

  • A working prototype

    An AI-enabled prototype, a sector map and a validated problem that you can show co-founders, investors and employers.

  • A room of serious people

    Demo Night and flagship events with founders, investors and engineers from the companies we work with.

Why BQ

Built to last past the first event.

Most student programmes depend on booking a speaker for each event. BQ runs on four things that are much harder to copy.

ExposureBuilder’s QuorumVenture
  1. Problems from communities we runWe run Claude Developers India, a practitioner community of more than 1,000 AI builders, and ML Reading Club Delhi NCR. Founders and engineers bring their problems to these communities every week, and those problems become the work in each sprint.
  2. Engineers as well as speakersEngineers from large technology companies, AI labs and startups in our networks run workshops and mentor pods directly, so you learn from the people who build the products.
  3. Continuity by designPods, fixed-length sprints, mentor follow-ups and Demo Night keep people building between sessions, which is where most communities lose them.
  4. A network that compoundsEvery cohort adds Fellows, mentors and partner companies, and each new cohort can reach all of them.

FAQ

Questions

What is Builder’s Quorum?

Builder’s Quorum (BQ) is a builder programme for ambitious students and early builders in India. It turns live problems from frontier sectors such as AI, fintech and real estate into build sprints, run with the founders and operators who own those problems.

Who is Builder’s Quorum for?

Ambitious students and early builders, aged 18 and over, from management, engineering and technology. You can join through a partner college or apply directly. It suits people who want to build something real, whether that is software, a consumer product or hardware.

Where does Builder’s Quorum run?

We are starting in Delhi NCR, with partner colleges and with individual builders who apply directly.

How is BQ different from a hackathon or case competition?

Those last a day or a weekend. BQ runs over weeks, with the same pod and the same practitioners, so you see how a sector actually works and leave with a working prototype.

How do I get a place?

Apply on this site. Cohorts are small. We look at what you are working towards, what you have built or led, and whether you can commit for the length of a sprint. You don’t need an idea yet.

How are BQ Fellows chosen?

Fellows are selected from inside a cohort rather than by application. A panel of founders and operators decides, based on what each person built, how consistently they showed up and what their pod says about working with them.

How can a college, investor or company get involved?

Colleges start with a pilot sprint. Angel and pre-seed investors see the strongest teams first. Companies and practitioners can bring a live problem, lend their engineers, back an event or hire the builders they meet. Reach out through the apply page.
