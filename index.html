Most tech events in India stay at the surface. Builder’s Quorum puts the people doing the research in front of a small room and lets the conversation go as deep as it needs to.

An evening

One problem, at research depth.

Each evening takes one problem from one field. It runs in a café or coworking space, and the room stays small.

  • A frontier problem

    A professor or researcher takes the room through a problem from their current work, at the depth they would use with colleagues.

  • Time for real questions

    The talk is short and the questions run for as long as the room wants. Nobody pitches.

  • A room worth meeting

    PhD and master’s researchers, engineers and technology professionals, with a few founders and investors there to listen.

  • Food and coffee on the table

    Evenings run in cafés and coworking spaces. Order something, and stay after the talk.

  • A fee you spend on dinner

    A reservation fee holds your seat, and all of it is redeemable against food and drink at the venue.

  • People who stay in touch

    Every evening adds to a network of researchers, engineers and investors that keeps meeting between evenings.

Why BQ

Built to last past the first evening.

Most tech events depend on booking a name and filling a hall. BQ runs on four things that are much harder to copy.

ResearchBuilder’s QuorumWhat gets built
  1. Speakers at research depthProfessors and researchers bring the problem they are working on now. We ask them to take it to the level they would use with colleagues, and we keep the room small enough for that to work.
  2. Communities we runWe run Claude Developers India, a practitioner community of more than 1,000 AI builders, and ML Reading Club Delhi NCR. That is where our speakers, our rooms and our problems come from.
  3. A room that is chosenPlaces are earned through an invitation or a short request, so the person next to you is worth meeting. The fee only holds a seat once you have one.
  4. A network that compoundsEvery evening adds speakers, researchers, engineers and investors. University chapters will give students a way into the same network.

How BQ grows

First the room, then the students.

We are building in a deliberate order, and each stage depends on the one before it.

  1. Evenings, starting nowSmall technical evenings in Delhi NCR for researchers, engineers and technology professionals, led by professors and researchers. This is where the network forms.
  2. University chapters, nextStudents join through a BQ chapter at their university, in cohorts. They work in pods on sector tracks, reach the speakers, engineers and investors from the evenings, and keep their place by showing up.
  3. More cities, after thatBengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad and Pune, with sponsors, once the format works in Delhi NCR.

FAQ

Questions

What is Builder’s Quorum?

Builder’s Quorum (BQ) is an independent community in India that runs small, technical evenings in Delhi NCR. At each one, a professor or researcher takes a room of PhD and master’s researchers, engineers and technology professionals deep into one frontier problem. University chapters for students come next.

Who are the evenings for?

PhD and master’s researchers, and engineers and professionals working in technology. A few founders and investors come to each evening to listen. The evenings are not open to undergraduates, who will be able to join through university chapters.

How do I get a seat?

A speaker or a member can invite you, or you can request a seat on this site. We read what you work on and what you would bring to the room. Rooms are small, so not every request gets a seat at the next evening.

Why is there a fee?

The fee holds your seat, so the people who book are the people who turn up. All of it is redeemable against food and drink at the venue on the night.

Where do the evenings happen?

In cafés and coworking spaces across Delhi NCR. The venue comes with your invitation. Other cities follow once the format works here.

I’m a professor or researcher. Can I lead an evening?

Yes. Tell us what you are working on. We find the venue, choose the room and handle invitations and bookings. You bring one problem and take it as deep as you would with colleagues.

What about students?

Students will join through BQ chapters at their universities, in cohorts. They work in pods on sector tracks and reach the speakers, engineers and investors from the evenings. Places in a chapter are earned too, and students keep them by showing up.

How can a company, sponsor or investor get involved?

Companies and sponsors can back an evening, help take the evenings to a new city or send a speaker. Investors can join the room and meet the people working on frontier problems. Reach out through the request page.
