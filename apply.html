Who can apply

From any college, or none.

Students and early builders, 18 and over, building software, consumer products or hardware. You don’t need an idea yet. We care more about what you have built or led than where you study.

What happens next

Cohorts are small.

We review applications before each cohort. If there is a fit, we set up a short call. Not everyone gets in, and we will tell you either way.
