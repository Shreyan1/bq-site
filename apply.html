Who comes

Researchers and professionals.

PhD and master’s researchers, engineers and technology professionals, with one speaker and a few founders and investors. Undergraduates join through university chapters.

What happens next

Rooms are small.

We read every request before each evening. If there is a seat, your invitation names the topic, the speaker, the venue and the reservation fee, which you spend in full at the venue. Not everyone gets a seat, and we will tell you either way.

What happens next

A short call first.

We reply to every offer, talk through the problem you want to take on and find a date and a room that suit it.

Chapters

Students join through chapters.

Chapters open after the first evenings in Delhi NCR. Tell us where you study and what you want to build, and we will write when there is a chapter you can join. Places are earned, and kept by showing up.

What happens next

A short note is enough.

We read every message and come back with something specific.
