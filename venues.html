Questions

About these venues.

Where can I host a tech meetup in Delhi NCR?

This page lists 6 spaces across Delhi NCR that host tech meetups, hackathons and workshops, including colleges, coworking spaces and company offices. Each listing shows capacity, terms, the nearest metro and how to contact the host.

Do these venues charge?

Every space is either a venue partner or paid. A venue partner hosts in return for something it values, such as its name on the event or places for its students. A paid venue rents the space, often at a community rate.

How do I book a space?

Contact the host directly with the email, phone or booking link on the listing.

How do I list my space?

Fill in the short venue form. We confirm the details with you before the listing goes up, and you can change or remove it at any time.
