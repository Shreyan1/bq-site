How booking works

You pick the space, and we make the ask.

We know the people who run each space, so you don’t have to cold-message anyone.

  1. Choose a spaceFilter by area and size. Each listing says what the venue asks in return.
  2. Tell us about your eventWhat it is, who it is for, the date and how many people you expect.
  3. We confirm with the venueWe ask on your behalf and come back to you, usually within two working days.
  4. You run the eventYou host, and the venue gets an evening of builders. Events booked through us name Builder’s Quorum as venue partner.

For venues

Host builders in your space.

  • Organisers we have spoken to

    Every request comes through us. We check who is running the event, what it is and who is coming before we bring it to you.

  • An audience worth having

    Engineers, founders and student builders in your space. For a company that means people you may want to hire, and for a college it means practitioners in front of your students.

  • Your terms, agreed up front

    You set the dates, the size and what you ask in return, whether that is a fee to cover costs, co-branding or places for your students.

  • Your details stay private

    We never publish phone numbers or emails. You hear from us, and only about events that suit your space.
