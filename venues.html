How it works

Every listing tells you what the host wants.

Every space is either a venue partner or paid. We check each listing with the venue before it goes up, and recheck it every three months.

  • Venue partner

    The space comes in return for something the host values, such as their name on the event, places for their students or time with your audience. The listing says what they ask for.

  • Paid

    The space is rented, often at a community rate. Some paid spaces can be booked online straight from the listing.

  1. Find a spaceFilter by area, size and terms, or look at the open dates that venues have posted.
  2. Talk to the hostEmail the host from the listing. The message is set up with the details they need, so you only fill in your event.
  3. Agree and hostDates, terms and the run of the day are agreed between you and the venue.
