What a session is

Closer to a lab meeting than a talk.

Each session is built around one person and one problem. The questions are the point, so the room stays small.

  1. 01

    One open problem

    The speaker brings something they haven’t solved yet: where it stands, what has failed and what would change if it worked. There is no survey of the field and no company pitch.

  2. 02

    A room that can follow

    PhD and master’s researchers, engineers and technology professionals. Everyone is there because they asked to be and we thought they would add to it.

  3. 03

    What happens after

    People in the room decide who they want to work with next. Some go on to set problems for, and mentor, the student teams in BQ’s university chapters.

Questions

Before you ask.

Who are the sessions for?

Researchers, engineers and technology professionals who want to go further into a frontier problem than a talk or a meetup allows. PhD and master’s researchers are welcome, and so are people building in industry or running their own company.

Why is there a contribution?

It covers the session and everything that comes with it. It also means every seat goes to someone who will turn up, which matters when the room is small.

What if I’m not selected?

We tell you, and your request stays with us for the next session. If you paid half to be prioritised, it is refunded in full.

Where and when are the sessions?

In Delhi NCR, at weekends. We choose the area, day and time from what people tell us in this form, and the invitation gives the venue, the date and the speaker.

Can I bring someone?

Invitations are personal, so each person requests their own seat. Once you’ve sent yours, you get a link to share, and we’ll see who came through you.
