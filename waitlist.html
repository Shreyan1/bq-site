Questions

Before you apply.

Who can apply?

Anyone who builds: engineers, designers, product people, analysts, consultants, marketers and operators, whether they are students, working professionals or early founders.

Do I need a team?

You can apply on your own or as a team of two to four. BQ forms mixed teams of tech and business builders from the people it selects.

Do I need to be technical?

Business builders are as welcome as engineers. Showing that a problem is real and that someone will pay takes both.

How much does it cost?
