Your students, inside live sectors.

What your campus gets

  • Practitioners and engineers who stay for the whole sprint
  • Your strongest teams at Demo Night

What we need from you

  • A student lead who owns it on campus

How it starts

Start with a pilot, then decide.

There are no fixed session counts and no long contracts up front.

  1. We listen firstWe learn what your students want, what you have tried and what worked. The pilot is shaped around that.
  2. One pilot sprintWe run a sprint with a committed group of your students, on problems from live sectors.
  3. Review togetherAttendance, prototypes and what students said. We share the numbers, good or bad.
  4. Decide what’s nextKeep going, change the format or stop.

Who runs BQ

Run by people who build.

BQ is run by practitioners working across AI, finance, real estate and enterprise technology, who also run builder and research communities.

  • Depth across sectors

    Each track is led by people who work in that sector.

  • Communities we run

    Builder, founder and research communities in Delhi NCR and online across India, which is where the problems come from.

  • Engagement between sessions

    Pods, labs and mentor follow-through keep the work going between sessions.

  • Technical and responsible-AI depth

    Prototypes are built with responsible AI practice from the start.

  • A network to take work further

    Founders, businesses and institutions who can open the next door.

  • Go-to-market, practised

    Selling, marketing and finding customers are part of every sprint.
