I’m an investor

Straight talk

Teams are early, and we say so.

BQ teams come out of a sprint at idea or prototype stage. That makes BQ a fit for angels and pre-seed funds. For Series A investors and beyond, the value today lies in mentoring, judging and shaping the pipeline rather than in writing cheques.

  • Angel and pre-seed

    Invest early

    See BQ Fellows and Demo Night teams before anyone else. Introductions only when a team is ready, and only with your say-so.

  • Any stage

    Mentor a pod

    A single session or an hour a week. Show teams how investors actually think about a market.

  • Any stage

    Judge Demo Night

    Give the strongest teams honest feedback in the room.

  • Series A and later

    Bring portfolio problems

    Your portfolio companies bring live problems, and meet builders they may want to hire.

How teams reach you

Introductions are warm, selective and on your terms.

  1. Selected inside a cohortTeams and Fellows are chosen by founders and operators for what they built, rather than on a pitch deck alone.
  2. Matched to fitWe introduce a team only to the few investors it fits. We don’t send mass emails or forward decks in bulk.
  3. Your termsYou decide what you want to see, which sectors and how often.
