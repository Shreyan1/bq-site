For investors / Angel, pre-seed and deep tech

Be in the room early.

For angel, pre-seed and deep-tech investors who want to meet researchers and engineers working on frontier problems, and for later-stage investors who want to help shape what comes out of that room.

I’m an investor

Straight talk

Nobody pitches at an evening.

The evenings are for understanding hard problems and meeting the people working on them. If someone in the room is building something, we introduce you afterwards, when both sides want it. Student teams from university chapters will be at idea or prototype stage, which suits angels and pre-seed funds.

  • Any stage

    Join an evening

    Sit in on a talk at research depth and meet the researchers and engineers in the room.

  • Angel and pre-seed

    Invest early

    Meet people as they start building, and later the strongest chapter teams and BQ Fellows. Introductions only when a team is ready, and only with your say-so.

  • Any stage

    Mentor and judge

    Mentor a chapter pod or judge Demo Night, and show teams how investors actually think about a market.

  • Series A and later

    Bring portfolio problems

    Your portfolio companies bring live problems to an evening or a chapter, and meet people they may want to hire.

How introductions work

Introductions are warm, selective and on your terms.

  1. Chosen roomsEveryone at an evening has earned a seat, and chapter teams and Fellows are chosen for what they built, rather than on a pitch deck alone.
  2. Matched to fitWe introduce someone only to the few investors they fit. We don’t send mass emails or forward decks in bulk.
  3. Your termsYou decide what you want to see, which sectors and how often.
