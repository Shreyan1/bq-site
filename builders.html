How it works

Live problems, mixed pods, working prototypes.

  • Live problems

    Founders, engineers and operators in the communities we run bring real problems from their work, so you never start from a blank page.

  • Mentors who build

    Engineers, founders and operators mentor pods directly and run workshops, from the first session to Demo Night.

  • Mixed pods

    Management and engineering students work as one team, so every pod has someone who can build and someone who can sell.

  • A prototype every time

    Every pod ships an AI-enabled prototype rather than a slide deck. AI is also used on the business side, for research, lead generation, marketing and pitch preparation.

One sprint, four stages

Each stage leaves you with something you can show.

  1. Discover

    Pick a sector, size the market and talk to the people inside it.

    You leave withA sector map and candidate problems

  2. Build

    Validate one problem with real users, form your team and build.

    You leave withA validated problem, a team and a prototype

  3. Fund

    Learn how ventures like yours get funded, from grants to venture capital.

    You leave withA funding map and a financial model

  4. Grow

    Find first customers, tell the story and pitch it to serious people.

    You leave withA first outreach list and your Demo Night pitch

Four formats, all hands-on

We only run sessions that are worth giving up a Sunday afternoon for, and none of them are lectures.

  • Frontier Briefing

    A practitioner explains how a sector makes money, where it is stuck and what is changing.

  • Build Lab

    A hands-on workshop, often run by an engineer, where you leave with something that works.

  • Founder Fireside

    Students host the conversation with a founder, come with prepared questions and agree a follow-up.

  • Demo Night

    The strongest teams present their work to an invited room of founders and investors.

BQ Fellows

The strongest builders from every cohort.

You can’t apply to be a Fellow. Fellows are chosen from inside each cohort for what they build, wherever they study.

How Fellows are chosen

  • What you built and shipped during the sprint
  • How consistently you showed up
  • What your pod says about working with you
  • A final call by a panel of founders and operators

What Fellows get

  • One-to-one time with founders and operators who have done it
  • Introductions to angel and pre-seed investors, when your venture is ready
  • A Demo Night slot in front of founders and investors
  • Introductions across our network, for ventures and for roles

Fellowships stay small on purpose. If a cohort doesn’t produce a Fellow, we don’t name one.

Proven formats

Borrowed from the best, built for India.

We adapt formats that leading programmes use to build real capability, and ground them in how India’s markets, customers and capital work.

  1. Stanford GSB, View From The TopStudents interview the leaders instead of a moderator. Our Founder Firesides are hosted by students, who prepare with a brief and a rehearsal.
  2. MIT Sloan, Action LearningStudent teams work on live problems with companies. Our pods take on live problems from our communities and hand over usable work.
  3. Stanford GSB, Startup GarageCustomer discovery first, then a prototype, then the pitch. Our Build Labs follow the same order.
  4. Oxford Saïd, Creative Destruction LabBusiness students work alongside deep-tech founders. BQ Fellows work with frontier-tech founders from our network.

BQ is independent and not affiliated with these programmes.
