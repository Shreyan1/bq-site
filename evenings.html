How it works

How an evening works.

  1. Invitation

    A speaker or a member invites you, or you request a seat and we read what you work on.

  2. Booking

    Your invitation names the topic, the speaker, the venue and the fee. The fee holds your seat, and you spend all of it at the venue.

  3. The evening

    One speaker and one problem, at research depth. Then questions, for as long as the room wants.

  4. After

    The room stays in touch. We make introductions when both sides want them, and people who keep showing up keep being invited.

Who is in the room

  • Researchers

    PhD and master’s researchers in any field that touches technology.

  • Engineers and professionals

    People building and shipping technology in industry, at startups and at large companies.

  • A speaker

    A professor or researcher from a university or lab, one for each evening.

  • Founders and investors

    A few at each evening, there to listen and to meet people working at the frontier.
